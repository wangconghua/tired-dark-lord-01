One original 100-word monologue for a fantasy villain who is secretly just
very tired. The tiredness is never announced — no "tired", "weary" or
"exhausted" anywhere — it lives in the rhythm: trailing off mid-sentence,
losing the thread, forgetting the hero's name, filing the apocalypse under
taxes. The villain still has a history (his speechwriter died of old age
waiting) and a scheme (an eclipse rite needing the hero's blood, sword and
name), and the dreaded kneel-or-die moment collapses into an invitation to
sit down.

Run `python3 main.py` to write the entry to `/output/entry.txt`
(100 words, 530 characters including the trailing newline — inside the
gate's 1–600 character limit).
